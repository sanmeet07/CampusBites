About CampusBites

The Problem

Students often do not know the menus, prices or timings of the food stalls on campus. They waste time walking to stalls that are closed, or keep going to the same place because they do not know what else is available.

Our Solution

CampusBites lists every food stall on campus in one place with:

  • Menus with prices
  • Opening and closing timings
  • Location on campus
  • A page to compare the same item at different stalls
  • Ratings and reviews from students
